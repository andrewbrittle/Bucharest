SURVIVAL PHRASES

Bulgarian

Cyrillic script — every phrase spelled out phonetically too
How to read it — the transliteration is the important bit, since you can't sound out Cyrillic on sight. Stress is marked in CAPS.
х = "ch" (loch) ъ = flat "uh" ж = "zh" (measure) ш = "sh"
