Romanian

Latin script — read it straight, with a few sounds to know
How to read it — Romanian is spelled the way it sounds, once you know five letters:
ă = "a" in "about" â / î = flat "uh" ș = "sh" ț = "ts" ce/ci = "che/chi" ghe/ghi = hard "ge/gi"
